# YOYO LIVE — Financial safety and pricing gates

Status: INTERNAL DESIGN / NOT APPROVED FOR REAL-MONEY SALES

This document is the required control checklist before enabling coin sales, cash-outs, or changing gift economics. No prices or payout conversion rates are approved by this document.

## Current verified constraints

- Gift transfers debit the sender's purchased coins and split gift points 70% to the host and 30% to the platform.
- The 70/30 split is currently expressed in points in the gift ledger. It is **not proof** of a 70/30 split of Egyptian-pound revenue or cash profit.
- The wallet has EGP earned/pending balance fields, but the inspected gift-transfer function credits host points, not EGP balances.
- Coin purchase approval currently credits coins only after an administrator approves a request and records a transaction. A transfer reference is not independent proof of settlement; the administrator must verify funds actually arrived and were not reversed before approval.
- No payment processor, processor fee, tax treatment, refund reserve, chargeback cost, or point-to-EGP redemption rate has been confirmed.
- Therefore real-money coin sales, cash-outs, and public coin package prices must remain disabled until the inputs below are known and tested.

## Required inputs for each coin package

Record and verify:
1. Customer price in EGP.
2. Coins delivered, including any bonus coins.
3. Payment method and its exact percentage fee, fixed fee, settlement fee, and refund/chargeback fees.
4. Any app-store commission if the purchase occurs in-app.
5. Applicable taxes and whether displayed prices include them.
6. Expected refund/chargeback reserve.
7. Infrastructure and streaming cost allocation per paying user or transaction.
8. Host redemption rate in EGP per eligible point, payout fees, and minimum payout.
9. Whether promotional/free coins can generate redeemable host points (default must be NO unless funded).

## Calculation rules

Let:
- P = customer payment collected (EGP, excluding amounts not actually settled)
- F = payment/app-store fees
- T = taxes attributable to the sale
- R = refund and chargeback reserve
- O = allocated operating cost
- C = coins credited (paid coins + bonus coins)
- V = verified EGP value of one coin = P / C only after settlement and all package rules are fixed
- G = coins consumed by gifts
- H = eligible host points generated by those gifts
- E = verified EGP redemption value per host point

Then:
- Net package contribution before gift redemption = P - F - T - R - O
- Revenue value consumed by a gift = G × V
- Host cash liability = H × E
- Platform contribution from a gift = (G × V) - (H × E) - any additional payout/servicing costs
- Overall contribution must be positive after all direct costs, taxes, reserves, payout obligations, and a safety margin.

Do not assume that one coin equals one point or that one point equals one EGP. Those are separate rates and require an explicit, funded conversion rule.

## Release gates (all required)

- [ ] Payment provider and settlement fees verified from the provider's current contract.
- [ ] Tax/accounting treatment reviewed for Egypt and any other launch countries.
- [ ] Coin package prices and bonus coins calculated with a positive contribution margin.
- [ ] Coin-to-point and point-to-EGP rates explicitly defined and stress-tested.
- [ ] 70/30 allocation tested in EGP, not only in points.
- [ ] Free/promotional coins prevented from creating unfunded cash liabilities, or funded by a documented marketing budget.
- [ ] Duplicate payment references, replayed approvals, partial payments, reversals, refunds, and chargebacks tested.
- [ ] Withdrawal requests reserve available funds and cannot be paid twice.
- [ ] Stress tests pass for high-gift users, high-host concentration, processor fee increases, and refund spikes.
- [ ] Admin audit trail and daily reconciliation report verified.
- [ ] End-to-end tests pass in a non-production environment.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

1. Never credit coins based only on a screenshot or an unverified transfer reference.
2. Never let a client app set coin balances, point conversion rates, EGP values, or payout amounts.
3. Never approve a payment twice; payment provider transaction IDs/references must be unique and idempotent.
4. Never let hosts withdraw more than settled, available, reconciled funds.
5. Keep pending earnings separate from payable earnings until the refund/reversal risk window and reconciliation rules are satisfied.
6. If any required financial input is unknown, keep real-money purchase and cash-out disabled.
7. Review the economics before changing gift coin costs, gross points, host share, package bonuses, or payout rates.
